RESTRICTED SNAP GROSS-INCOME TEST TASK

Determine only whether the household passes the gross monthly income test for
this month, under this experimental packet. Do not decide overall SNAP
eligibility, deductions, net income or benefit amount.

Fixed, verified background in every case:
- Determination month is March 2026; all amounts are monthly.
- No household member is elderly (60 or older) or disabled, so the gross
  income test applies to this household.
- The household size stated in the verified record is established.
- Gross monthly income is the sum of earned income and unearned income.
- The household passes the test if gross monthly income is at or below the
  limit for its size: 1 person $1,696; 2 persons $2,292; 3 persons $2,888;
  4 persons $3,483; 5 persons $4,079; 6 persons $4,675.

Evidence:
- Only the verified record establishes income amounts.
- A field shown as UNKNOWN, or an income field that the verified record does
  not list, is not established: it is not zero.
- A statement by the applicant or by any other person that is not backed by a
  record on file does not establish an amount.
- Earned income and unearned income are each nonnegative whole-dollar amounts
  from 0 through 6,000.
- Preferred contact window has no bearing on this test.

Output:
- MEETS: every completion consistent with the verified record passes the test.
- FAILS: every such completion fails the test.
- NEEDS_INFO: some such completions pass the test and others fail it.

MEETS does not mean that the household has been approved for SNAP. NEEDS_INFO is
an experimental evidence-sufficiency label, not a real agency's disposition.

# Development audit: scope, gold and cue construction

2026-10-08. Computational and source-grounding audit; not an expert legal opinion
or a human-participant construct-validation study.

## Policy mapping

| Packet element | Primary-source support | Restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Combined work/work-program hours must reach 80 | USDA work-requirements page describes the combination route | Only paid work and approved-program hours vary here |
| Medical inability can excuse the time limit | USDA and current NC guidance describe health-related exemptions | The input supplies whether the documented exemption applies; the model does not diagnose it |
| Three countable months and an exception/work route | USDA describes the time-limit structure; NC describes screening and exceptions | Prior months fixed; all other routes explicitly excluded |
| Age 35, March 2026 | Experimental fixed case, within the relevant working-age range | No age-law-change claim |
| Missing fact is null, not false | Experimental evidence-sufficiency semantics | Not a statement of real agency verification/denial procedure |
| 0..160 integer hours per field | Declared completion domain | Not a legal upper bound or population distribution |

Sources read on 2026-10-08:

- https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/snap/work-requirements
- https://www.ncdhhs.gov/divisions/child-and-family-well-being/food-and-nutrition-services-food-stamps/able-bodied-adults-without-dependents-abawds

The USDA consumer page explicitly says it awaits updated guidance and still
lists older age/child/veteran criteria. Do NOT cite its whole exception list as
the current law. NC's current page describes changed age/child criteria. This
pilot fixes age and excludes those exception types rather than making a claim
about their updated boundaries. A full main-study audit must use applicable
statutes and implementation guidance for every varied rule.

NC also explains exemption screening, time to provide proof and good cause.
Those procedures are outside this experimental packet. An unverified medical
field is not sufficient grounds to assert that a real person should be denied.
The label NEEDS_INFO was chosen to avoid that conflation.

## Independent gold checks

- Hand-specified Boolean reference: medical exemption OR combined hours >= 80.
- Compare the existing complete-record engine on 98 combinations, including
  totals immediately around 80 and values above the 130-hour exception boundary.
- Check decisive missingness: unknown program hours with medical=false and
  known paid hours below 80 gives both passing and failing completions.
- Check redundant missingness: known medical=true remains MEETS with unknown
  hours; known total>=80 remains MEETS with unknown medical status.
- Check an irrelevant unknown contact window does not change the legal outcome.
- Check invalid types, negative hours and incomplete schemas fail validation.

All missingness labels are generated by exhaustive completion over the stated
domain. No model answer, first-token probability, maximum allotment or
researcher's preferred allocation is used as gold.

## Cue audit

| Manipulation | What changes | What is held fixed / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| High vs low work ethic | Unverified assessment of diligence/willingness | Same certified record, missing fields and gold |
| Positive vs negative folder opinion | Valence unrelated to legal variables | Valence intensity may differ from moral notes |
| Neutral reference | No evaluation of work ethic | May direct attention away from the same moral concept |
| Checklist vs narrative | Rendering of the SAME partial record | Two styles are not independent template corpora |

The cues make no claim about actual job applications, hours, dismissal, injury,
repair costs or program attendance. This avoids the specific legal/factual
leakage in the older design. They still operationalize a narrow work-ethic
description, not the entire deservingness construct.

Moral high/low word counts match within each of the three variants. Positive and
negative non-moral controls match each other, but are not perfectly length- and
intensity-matched to every moral pair. The paired interaction and non-moral
control reduce that concern without eliminating it. The main study needs
independent wording and a documented parallelism review.

The explicit unverified-note label and evidence instructions are deliberate
development scaffolding. They may overestimate resistance to cues compared with
natural case records. Do not extrapolate this easy setting to actual welfare
administration, or relax it selectively to obtain an effect.

## Claim audit

Currently supported: executable gold, deterministic development generation and
CPU-tested analysis/parser safeguards. Not supported yet: model competence,
the candidate cue-by-missingness interaction, mitigation effectiveness,
cross-family replication, actual harm or a 4.0 reviewer recommendation.
